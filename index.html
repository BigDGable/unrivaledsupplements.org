Process

The path every formula takes

Six stages, each with a written record and a sign-off before the next one starts.

  1. 01

    Evidence review

    We map the clinical research for each ingredient: effective doses, forms and safety limits.

  2. 02

    Formula design

    Ingredients, amounts and excipients are set in a written specification.

  3. 03

    Ingredient qualification

    Suppliers are vetted, and each raw material lot is tested for identity before use.

  4. 04

    Pilot batch

    A small-scale run confirms blend uniformity, stability and process settings.

  5. 05

    Production

    Full batches run against the master manufacturing record with in-process checks.

  6. 06

    Release

    Finished lots are tested against specification and released with a certificate of analysis.

Formats

Dosage forms we produce

Each format has its own formulation limits. We help you choose the one that fits the dose, the ingredients and how your customers will take it.

  • Capsules

    Gelatin or plant-based HPMC shells in standard sizes.

  • Tablets

    Coated, uncoated and chewable tablets for higher doses.

  • Softgels

    For oils and fat-soluble nutrients such as vitamin D and omega-3s.

  • Powders

    Bulk tubs and single-serve stick packs for large servings.

  • Gummies

    Pectin-based chewables for customers who avoid pills.

Standards

What we hold every product to

These rules apply to every formula we make, for every brand we make it for.

Human evidence first

Ingredient decisions rest on human clinical trials. Animal and cell studies can raise questions. Human trials set the dose.

Doses that match the research

Each ingredient is included at an amount supported by the studies behind it. Trace amounts added for a label line don't make the cut.

Full label disclosure

Every ingredient and its exact amount appear on the label. We don't use proprietary blends to hide quantities.

Tested before release

No lot ships until it passes identity, potency, purity and microbial testing against its written specification.
