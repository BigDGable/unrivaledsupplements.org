About us

A supplement company built on measurement.

Unrivaled Supplements was founded on a simple standard. Every claim needs research behind it, and every batch needs test results before it leaves our facility.

Mission

Our mission

To make dietary supplements that do what their labels say, in the amounts their labels state.

We serve supplement brands and the people who buy from them. Both should be able to trust what is in the bottle.

That trust comes from three habits we keep on every product: research before formulation, documentation during production and testing before release.

Values

What we value

Evidence

We follow the research, including when it rules out a popular ingredient.

Transparency

Partners see the specifications, batch records and test results for their products.

Accountability

Every step has an owner and a record. When something goes wrong, we find the cause and correct it.

Craft

Small details decide whether a formula still meets its label on the last day of its shelf life. We give them full attention.

Organization

How we're organized

Five teams share responsibility for every product, from the first literature search to the last shipment.

Research & Regulatory
Reviews the clinical literature, sets dose rationale and checks claim language.
Formulation Science
Builds and tests prototypes, runs stability studies and writes product specifications.
Manufacturing Operations
Runs production, maintains equipment and keeps batch records complete.
Quality Assurance
Tests materials and finished products, and approves every lot for release.
Partner Services
Manages projects, timelines and reorders for each brand we work with.
