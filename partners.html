What every partner gets

These come standard with every development and manufacturing engagement.

  • A dedicated project manager as your single point of contact
  • A written timeline with milestones for each phase
  • Formula and specification documents you approve before production
  • A certificate of analysis with every production lot
  • Label content review for required regulatory elements
  • Reorder planning to keep your inventory steady

Engagement

How an engagement runs

A typical development project moves through five phases.

  1. 01

    Introductory call

    We learn about your brand, your customers and the product you want to make.

  2. 02

    Feasibility and proposal

    We assess the formula, format and minimums, then send a written proposal.

  3. 03

    Development

    Research, formulation, prototypes and stability work, with your approval at each stage.

  4. 04

    First production run

    Your first lot is manufactured, tested and released with its certificate of analysis.

  5. 05

    Ongoing supply

    Scheduled reorders, continued testing and formula updates as your line grows.

FAQ

Common questions

Don't see your question? Our team can walk you through the details on an introductory call.

What are your minimum order quantities?

Minimums depend on the format and the complexity of the formula. We confirm them in the feasibility proposal so you can plan inventory and budget.

How long does development take?

Timelines depend on the formula and the stability work it needs. Your proposal includes a written schedule with milestones for each phase.

Can you manufacture a formula I already have?

Yes. Through formula transfer we review your existing specification, qualify ingredient sources and run a pilot batch before full production.

Do you work with new brands?

Yes. We work with brands at every stage, including companies launching their first product.

Who owns the formula?

Ownership terms are set out in your agreement before development begins.

Do you design labels and packaging artwork?

We review label content for regulatory requirements, including the Supplement Facts panel. Artwork design stays with your creative team or agency.
